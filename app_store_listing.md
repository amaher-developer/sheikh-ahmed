**Description**
```
"Sheikh Ahmed" is your daily companion for worship: prayer times and the adhan, the Holy Quran in the script of the printed Mus'haf, live Quran radio, azkar and duas, and a worship tracker — in one simple, elegant app, in Arabic and English.

Prayer & Adhan
• Accurate prayer times calculated on your device for your location or city, with 13 calculation methods and the Shafi'i and Hanafi madhabs
• A notification at every prayer time with the adhan voice you choose — Makkah, Madinah, Egyptian or Turkish — and the full adhan while the app is open
• Countdown to the next prayer, plus sunrise, midnight and the last third of the night
• A monthly imsakiya all year round, and a Ramadan mode with countdowns to iftar and imsak and taraweeh and qiyam times
• Qibla direction with a live compass

Holy Quran
• The 604-page Mus'haf, laid out like the printed copy (Arabic interface)
• Listen to 21 reciters, including Alafasy, Al-Sudais, Al-Shuraim, Abdulbasit, Al-Husary, Al-Minshawi, Al-Muaiqly and Al-Ghamdi
• Play from any verse, continuing automatically into the next surah
• Tafsir Al-Muyassar, colour-coded tajweed rules for each verse, and word-by-word listening
• Saheeh International English translation in the English interface
• Search the Quran's verses and jump by page, juz or hizb
• Bookmarks in three colours to organise memorisation and review, and continue reading where you left off
• Download the whole Mus'haf and surah recitations to read and listen offline
• Memorisation mode: hide the verse, recite from memory, record yourself, listen to the reciter, then reveal

Quran Radio
7 stations, including Quran Radio Cairo and Saudi Quran Radio, with background playback and lock-screen controls

Azkar & Duas
• 8 azkar categories: morning, evening, sleep, after prayer, ruqyah, waking up, food and the adhan — with a counter for each dhikr and daily progress
• 68 duas in 10 groups: forgiveness, repentance, provision, worry and hardship, health and healing, guidance, protection, ease and knowledge, family and parents, and the hereafter
• A digital tasbeeh with eight phrases and targets you choose

Worship Tracker
Track your five daily prayers and your streak, your Quran reading, morning and evening azkar, and night prayer

Weekly Mission & Verse of the Day
A practical good deed each week, and a verse or hadith every day that you can share

Zakat Calculator
Estimate the zakat due on your cash and gold against the nisab

Reminders
Morning and evening azkar, your daily wird, surah of the day and dhikr through the day — switch them off any time

Dark mode, with full Arabic and English support
```

## App Review Information — Notes (English)

```
Thank you for reviewing Sheikh Ahmed.

- No account or login is needed; every feature is available immediately.
- Language: the app opens in Arabic by default. To switch to English, open the last tab "المزيد" (More) → "لغة التطبيق" (App language) → English. The app reloads in English.
- Location is used only on the device, to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is optional: a preset city (Cairo by default) can be chosen in More → Location.
- Notifications: an adhan notification at each prayer time, plus optional daily reminders (azkar, daily Quran wird). Both can be switched off in More.
- Background audio: Quran recitations (Quran tab) and live Quran radio (Radio tab) keep playing in the background with lock-screen controls.
- Microphone: used only in Memorization mode (Quran tab → open a surah → Memorization button in the top bar) to record the user's own recitation for playback. Recordings stay on the device.
- Content sources: Quran text, tafsir and word data from public Quran APIs (alquran.cloud, quran.com); recitations from mp3quran.net and everyayah.com; live radio from qurango.net and radiojar.com.

Contact: sheikhahmed.app@gmail.com
```
