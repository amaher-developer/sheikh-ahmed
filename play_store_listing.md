### Full description

```
"Sheikh Ahmed" is your daily companion for worship — prayer times and adhan, the Holy Quran in multiple reciters, azkar, and a real worship tracker, all in one simple, elegant app.

🕌 Prayer & Adhan
• Accurate prayer times for your location, with multiple calculation methods and madhabs
• Automatic adhan notification at every prayer time
• Qibla direction with an accurate compass

📖 Holy Quran
• Mus'haf-style reading with authentic Uthmani script
• Listen to 6 renowned reciters (Alafasy, Al-Sudais, Al-Shuraim, Abdul Basit, Al-Husary, Al-Minshawi)
• Browse by swiping between surahs, by Juz, favorites, or saved bookmarks
• Tap any verse to start playback from exactly there
• Read offline after opening a surah once, and download audio per surah for offline listening
• English translation available

🎧 Quran Radio
Live Quran radio streams

🌙 Azkar
Morning, evening, sleep, and after-prayer azkar, with an interactive tasbih counter

✅ Worship Tracker
Track your daily prayers, night prayer, and Quran reading, with a weekly streak

🎯 Weekly Mission
A specific, actionable good deed each week — feeding those in need, charity, memorizing a surah, and more

🧠 Memorization Mode
Hide the ayah, recall it from memory, record yourself, listen to a reciter, then reveal to check

💰 Zakat Calculator
Calculate your zakat easily and accurately

🔔 Smart Reminders
Notifications for adhan and azkar, plus a home-screen widget with a rotating dhikr

🌓 Full dark mode support, in both Arabic and English

"Sheikh Ahmed" is designed to be your companion throughout the day — making worship easy and beautiful.
```

- **App name / title**: "الشيخ أحمد" / "Sheikh Ahmed" — confirm this is the final public name; Play Console titles are capped at 30 characters (both fit comfortably).
- **Screenshots**: Google Play requires at least 2 phone screenshots (up to 8) per listing language. None were generated here — you'll need to capture these from a real device/emulator run of the app.
- **Feature graphic**: a 1024×500 banner image is required for Play Store; not included here.
- **Privacy policy URL**: required by Play Console before publishing, since the app requests location, microphone, and notification permissions. Needs a hosted privacy policy page — not something this session can produce without a hosting destination.
- **Content rating questionnaire**: Play Console will ask about content categories; this app has no user-generated content, violence, or similar concerns, so it should score in the lowest/"Everyone" tier — but the questionnaire itself has to be filled out in Console directly.
